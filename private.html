Private yoga in Dénia

At your accommodation, your urbanisation, or the beach of your choice. Individual or couples, without sharing a mat with anyone else, at your own pace.

A philosophy

We don't come to repeat the same class for everyone.

A private class isn't the same class as always at a different time. It's something else: it's built around you, your body, and what you need that day.

It's not a generic class.
It's a class designed for you.
You don't have to come to us.
We come to wherever you are.
There's no fixed time slot.
We find the time that works best for you, together.
No prior experience needed.
All you need is curiosity.
Who is it for?

For those who want a space entirely their own.

  • For those who prefer to practise without sharing the space with anyone.
  • For couples looking for their own moment during their holiday.
  • For those with any discomfort or injury who need a practice adapted to their body.
  • For those staying far from Los Baños Sunshine who prefer the class to come to them.
  • For those who have never tried yoga and prefer to start in an intimate setting rather than a group.
Who we are

Two teachers.
One class for you.

We're Nati and Naylín. We've been teaching yoga for over a decade, trained in India and in Spain, with our own schools behind us. In a private class you get all of that experience at the service of just one person: you. No one else on the mat, nothing that isn't exactly what you need that day.

When and where

No fixed time slot.

We don't have a fixed schedule for private classes, we find the best time for you together, with a minimum of 12 hours notice so we can prepare properly.

You also choose the location: your urbanisation, your home, or the beach of your choice in Dénia.
