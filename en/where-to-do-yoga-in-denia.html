Where to do yoga in Dénia

The places we know and would send a friend to.

By Nati and Naylín · November 2026

People often ask us where to do yoga in Dénia. Sometimes the answer is "come to the beach on Saturday". And sometimes it isn't. Some people need to practise several days a week, some are looking for something more therapeutic, and some would rather have a quiet room when it's cold.

So here are the places we really know, and the people behind them. It isn't a list of everything in Dénia: these are places we'd send a friend. Under each one we've added a line from their students, taken from their Google reviews (in Spanish, translated).

Our class: beach yoga on Saturdays

Every Saturday morning we practise by the sea, usually at Los Baños Sunshine in Les Marines. One hour, all levels, taught in English and Spanish. €15. Now and then we move to another beach or change the time, and it's always on our upcoming classes page. If it rains, we go to Elena's studio in El Verger.

The places we know

Mångata Yoga Dénia

Marta · Dénia town centre

Marta has a warm studio on Calle Montcada. She combines yoga with osteopathy and really looks after each person. Her reviews mention a lot of pregnant women and people who arrive with aches and pains.

«A great professional, very attentive to each person's needs» — Google review

C/ Montcada 37, Dénia · mangatayogadenia.com

Ashtanga Yoga Dénia

Mateo · Dénia town centre

If you like a steady practice, Mateo teaches Mysore-style Ashtanga: everyone does their own sequence at their own pace and he guides you closely. He also teaches therapeutic yoga and mindfulness, and has online classes.

«Mateo adapts the practice to each student's condition and needs» — Google review

C. de Gata de Gorgos 10, Dénia · ashtangayogadenia.es

Respyra Yoga & Desarrollo Personal

Ale · Dénia town centre

Ale teaches Hatha, yoga nidra and meditation, and combines them with personal development and retreats. A good place if, as well as moving, you want to slow down and look inwards.

«I've been coming to this studio for over 3 years and I love it» — Google review

Carrer Consolat del Mar 3, Dénia · respyra.com

Aura Yoga Estudio

Elena · El Verger, 10 minutes from Dénia

This is Elena's studio, and also our refuge on rainy Saturdays. You feel calm as soon as you walk in.

«A really cosy, beautiful room to relax in» — Google review

Carrer Doctor Domenech 3, El Verger · see on Google Maps

If you're looking for yoga in English

You can follow our Saturday class entirely in English. There are also teachers in the area who work in English. We don't know them personally, but here they are in case they help:

  • YogaAlexi: Vinyasa, Power and Yin classes in several places around Dénia, Els Poblets and Xàbia. Website in English: yogaalexi.com.
  • Yoga Breaks in Spain: multi-day retreats in English at a hotel in Dénia: yogabreaks.org.uk.

And if you don't know where to start

Message us. Tell us what you're looking for, how many days you can practise and how you're feeling, and we'll honestly tell you where we think you'll be best. Even if it isn't with us.
