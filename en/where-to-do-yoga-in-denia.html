Where to do yoga in Dénia: a practical guide

On the beach or in a studio, in English or Spanish, in summer or winter. How to choose what really suits you.

By Nati and Naylín, teachers at Viva Community Yoga · Updated November 2026

In short

  • Beach yoga all year round: Viva Community Yoga runs a group class by the sea every Saturday morning, usually at Los Baños Sunshine (Les Marines). €15, all levels, taught in English and Spanish.
  • Weekday or studio classes: there are several studios in and around the centre of Dénia with daily timetables and different styles.
  • Yoga in English: our Saturday class is taught in English and Spanish, and other teachers in the area also teach in English.
  • Private class: at your home, holiday rental or a beach of your choice, from €65.

Dénia has plenty of yoga, and that's a good thing: everyone needs something different. We wrote this guide to help you choose. As local teachers, we also tell you where we fit in, and where we might not.

If you want to do yoga on the beach

Practising by the sea is one of the best things you can do in Dénia. Our English-speaking beach yoga class is every Saturday morning, all year round, usually at Los Baños Sunshine (Carrer Riu Grande 12, Les Marines beach). Now and then we move to another beach or change the time, so always check the upcoming classes.

It lasts one hour, it's for all levels and costs €15 (3-class pass €40, 5-class pass €60). If it rains, it isn't cancelled: we hold it at Aura Yoga Estudio in El Verger. See the beach yoga class.

In summer, Dénia town council often runs free outdoor sports activities on some beaches; check their programme each season.

If you want weekday or studio classes

If you'd like to practise several days a week, with morning and evening times, a studio is probably right for you. Dénia has several in and around the centre, with styles such as Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga or Yin. Search "yoga Dénia" on Google Maps, check their timetables and try a class before deciding.

In winter we only teach the Saturday class, so if you need more, combine it with a studio or with private classes.

If you're looking for yoga in English

Dénia is very international and there are several options in English. Our Saturday class is taught in English and Spanish, so you don't need to speak Spanish to join. Private classes can be fully in English too. See our FAQ about yoga in English in Dénia.

If you've never done yoga

Look for an all-levels class, with a small group and a teacher who gives you options for each posture. Our beach class is designed that way: many people have taken their very first yoga class with us. If you'd rather start with one-to-one attention, a private class is a good way in.

If you're here in winter

Yes, you can do yoga outdoors in Dénia in winter. Mornings are fresh, the sea is usually calm and the beach is almost empty. Bring a warm layer for the start and end, and a towel or mat. Our Saturday class runs all year.

If you want a private class

To practise at your own pace, as a couple or with family on holiday, a private class is easiest: we come to your home, villa, holiday rental or a beach of your choice in Dénia and nearby. €65 for one person and €80 for two. See private classes.

If you want to go deeper

If you've been practising for a while and want to take yoga beyond the mat, we run Yoga For Life, an eight-month training with monthly sessions in Jávea.

Three questions to help you choose

  • How many days a week do you want to practise? One: the Saturday beach class. Several: a studio.
  • Does language matter? If you prefer English, ask first; we teach in both.
  • What are you after: moving, switching off or going deeper? Tell us, and if we think somewhere else suits you better, we'll say so.
