Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In accordance with article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 on information society services (LSSI), Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD), we explain clearly which cookies and similar technologies this website uses, why, and how you can manage them.

1. Who is responsible

This website is run by Naylín Núñez Gómez (Viva Community Yoga). You can write to us at vivacommunityyoga@gmail.com. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser when you visit it. They are used, for example, to remember your preferences or to understand how the site is used. Similar technologies exist, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), which work in a similar way and are also covered by this policy.

3. What we use on this website

Ours is a simple website: we currently do not use any analytics, advertising or social media cookies. We only use what is strictly necessary for the site to work.

Third-party services loaded on the website

  • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.): provides the website's fonts. It does not set cookies, but to download the fonts your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.

Links to other websites

We link to WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps and our Google profile. These platforms do not set cookies on this website: they will only do so if you click through and visit them, under their own policies. Payments are made on the secure platforms of Stripe and PayPal, which have their own cookie policies.

Analytics and advertising cookies

Today none are active, although the cookie notice already lets you choose about them. If we use them in the future (for example, to measure visits), they will only be activated with your consent, and we will update this page with the details of each one.
