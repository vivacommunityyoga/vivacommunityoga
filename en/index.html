Who is this for?

If you came here looking for yoga in Dénia, we understand.

Maybe you've never taken a class and have no idea where to start.

Maybe what you need is simply to stop. To leave the noise of the week behind, breathe, and give yourself an hour to come back to yourself.

Maybe you're visiting Dénia and don't want to stop practising while you're here.

Or maybe you've been doing yoga for years and feel ready to go beyond the postures: to understand better what happens in your mind, how you relate to others, what you do with what you feel, and how to bring all of that into your life.

You don't need to have it figured out.
You can come for any of these reasons.

Come to practise. Notice how you feel. And discover what happens when you make space for yourself.

And maybe we're not for you.

And that's okay too.

There are many teachers and spaces to practise yoga in Dénia. We know quite a few of them, and we believe every person needs something different.

So if, after getting to know you, we think another practice, teacher, or even another space might suit you better, we'll tell you.

We don't just want you to find a yoga class.
We want you to find the yoga that makes sense for you.

Who we are

Two teachers.
One shared space.

We're Nati and Naylín. We've been teaching yoga for over a decade, and this summer we share it here, by the sea. We trained in India and in Spain, and have taught in our own schools, at conferences, in very different places. And this (a mat, the sea ahead, sharing it with you) is what we love. It brings us back to simplicity, to the origin. If you come, even just once, you'll already be part of our family, of this Community that wants to enjoy that hour without demands, without phones, without rushing.

Yoga in Dénia

Yoga in Dénia, all year round.

Viva Community Yoga is a yoga space in Dénia where you can practise all year, in a weekly class, in a private session, or in our monthly Deep Yoga & Brunch.
