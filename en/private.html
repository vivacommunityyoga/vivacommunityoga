A philosophy

We're not here to repeat the same class for everyone.

A private class isn't the usual class at a different time. It's something else: it's built around you, your body and what you need that day.

It's not a generic class.
It's a class designed for you.
You don't have to come to us.
We come to wherever you are.
There's no fixed timetable.
Together we find the time that suits you best.
No previous experience needed.
Just curiosity.
Who is it for?

For those who want a space entirely their own.

  • For those who prefer to practise without sharing the space with anyone.
  • For couples looking for their own moment during their holiday.
  • For those with any discomfort or injury who need a practice adapted to their body.
  • For those staying far from Los Baños Sunshine who prefer the class to come to them.
  • For those who have never tried yoga and prefer to start in an intimate setting rather than a group.
Who we are

Two teachers.
One class for you.

We're Nati and Naylín. We've been teaching yoga for over a decade, trained in India and in Spain, and have run our own schools. In a private class, all that experience is at the service of one person: you. No one else on the mat, nothing but what you need that day.

When and where

No fixed timetable.

There is no set timetable for private classes — together we find the best time for you, with a minimum of 12 hours notice so we can prepare properly.

You also choose the location: your urbanisation, your home, or the beach of your choice in Dénia.
