One shared inquiry

This autumn, we didn't want to fill a calendar.

We created a journey. A class can leave a sensation in the body. A conversation can leave a question in your head. A Deep can open a door. And Yoga For Life lets you walk through it for eight months.

The yoga tradition puts the maps on the table. Practice lets us experience them. And life tells us what happens when we bring them home.

This season's centrepiece

Yoga For Life

8 months · 10 spots · first edition

There's a moment in practice when a posture stops being just a posture. You start noticing how demand shows up, how you compare, how you run from an uncomfortable sensation.

We don't want to give you a collection of answers. We want to help you develop the ability to find your own.
