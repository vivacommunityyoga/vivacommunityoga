Current season

Autumn at Los Baños Sunshine, Dénia.

From 19 September to 19 December. Every Saturday has its own format: a one-hour class to come back to your body, breathe, and practise, or a three-hour Deep Yoga & Brunch with breakfast and a workshop. All levels, no experience needed. See the full autumn calendar →

Four dates this season
Deep Yoga & Brunch

26 September, 17 October, 14 November and 5 December, 8:00–11:00am. On these Saturdays there's no regular class: a slower, deeper practice, followed by breakfast and a workshop that starts from a different concept from the yoga tradition each month, following the thread of what we're exploring that month in Yoga For Life.
